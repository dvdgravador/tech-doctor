Tech Doctor - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is applicable to the Tech Doctor app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Texto de Ejemplo (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

User Provided Information

The Application does not require registration and does not ask for any personal information. The values you type into the calculators (for example water hardness, pool volume, power or pressures) are stored only on your own device, so that you do not have to type them again the next time you open the Application. This information is never sent to the Service Provider or to anyone else.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application does not collect any information automatically. It does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking services, and it works fully offline.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not gather any information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No. The Application does not transmit any data to the Service Provider or to external services, and it does not include third-party services that collect data.

The Application is distributed through Google Play, which has its own Privacy Policy about handling data:

  • Google Play

What are my opt-out rights?

The Application does not collect information, so there is nothing to opt out of. You can delete the values stored on your device at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application using the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace.

Data Retention Policy, Managing Your Information

The Service Provider does not retain any data from the Application, because no data leaves your device. The values stored locally remain on your device until you clear the Application's data or uninstall it. If you have any question about your information, please contact the Service Provider at texto-ejemplo@googlegroups.com and we will respond in a reasonable time.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about the Application, please contact the Service Provider (texto-ejemplo@googlegroups.com).

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application does not collect or transmit any data, the values you enter never leave your device and are protected by your device's own security.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

Contact us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about the practices, please contact the Service Provider via email at texto-ejemplo@googlegroups.com.
